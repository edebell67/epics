What we do

Four disciplines that
compound on each other.

Most agencies hand you a website and leave. We build the site, the assistant that answers on it, the search presence that fills it, and the data systems that tell you whether any of it worked.

01

Web design & rebuilds

Bespoke sites for businesses that have outgrown a template — and rescue work for the ones stuck on something a decade old. Hand-built, fast, and owned by you.

  • Design systems, not one-off page layouts
  • Sub-second loads on hand-written HTML and CSS
  • Accessible by default — semantics, contrast, focus states
  • Legacy migration with content and rankings preserved

What we build toward A site you own outright, built to load fast and turn more of your visitors into enquiries.

02

AI site assistants

A trained assistant that lives on your site, answers real questions in your voice, books the job, and tells your team when a lead has gone cold instead of letting it die.

  • Grounded in your actual services, prices and hours
  • Lead capture with automatic follow-up on non-converters
  • Admin dashboard with recovered-revenue reporting
  • Modular — switch capabilities on per client

What we build toward Enquiries answered at 11pm, and a dashboard that puts a figure on what follow-up brought back.

03

Local SEO & lead generation

Being findable in the ten miles that actually matter. Technical foundations, local signals, and an outbound engine that finds businesses before they start looking.

  • Technical SEO, structured data and Core Web Vitals
  • Local pack positioning and review infrastructure
  • AI-native discovery — built to be cited, not just crawled
  • Automated prospect discovery and qualification

What we build toward A steady inbound line instead of a monthly scramble for the next job.

04

Trading & data products

The deep end. Live market data pipelines, strategy execution engines, backtest-to-live parity auditing, and the analytics layer that makes any of it trustworthy.

  • Real-time ingestion and multi-asset price collection
  • Strategy execution with full audit trails
  • Backtest and live parity verification
  • Analytics dashboards over SQL and PostgreSQL

What we build toward Systems that keep their promises after the demo is over.

How we work

Three moves.
No theatre.

01

Diagnose

We look at what you have, what it costs you, and what it's failing to do. You get a straight assessment — including when the answer is that you don't need us.

02

Build

Hand-built, in the open, with something to look at early. No six-week silence followed by a reveal. You see it as it becomes real.

03

Prove

Shipped with the instrumentation to show whether it worked. Load times, enquiries, conversions, recovered leads — evidence, not assurances.

The principle

Technology should
earn its place.

The name isn't decoration. Every choice we make on your project has to justify itself against a simpler alternative — and most of the time, the simpler thing wins.

  1. Build it, don't assemble it

    Hand-written code we understand end to end, instead of a stack of plugins nobody can debug at 2am.

  2. Evidence over assertion

    If we say it's faster, there's a number. If we say it converts, there's a dashboard showing it.

  3. You own the outcome

    Your code, your data, your accounts. No hostage-taking, no proprietary lock-in, no rental model on your own site.

  4. Say no to the wrong work

    We turn down projects we can't do well. A short honest conversation beats a long expensive mistake.
