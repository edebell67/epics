Owner review — per-page capture: delivery status

Every item is independently verifiable and carries an explicit state. Nothing is marked Passed from a local build alone where the item requires live or device evidence. Workflow source of truth: EP047_owner_review_per_page_capture_workflow.html (v1.9.0).

Two gates come before any code. Both can invalidate work done ahead of them, so they are not ordinary checklist items. The discreteness audit determines the shape of the config; the body-length measurement determines whether mailto: survives as the delivery mechanism at all. Building the engine before either is answered risks rewriting it.
