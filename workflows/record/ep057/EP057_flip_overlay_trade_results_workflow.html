EP057 · INTERACTIVE WORKFLOW

Opposite-side trade comparison

The actual signal remains authoritative. The flip is an observation of the opposite exposure over exactly the same interval.

1 · Actual entry

Take the selected BUY or SELL signal.

2 · Fixed interval

Preserve its entry and exit timestamps.

3 · Flip side

BUY → SELL; SELL → BUY for the same strategy.

4 · Compare

Display actual and flip results per row and in totals.

Availability rule

Buy/Sell split and Tri-Split trades have explicit sides and receive a flip result. Total Net leader rotation has no explicit side, so the flip result is shown as unavailable rather than inferred.

Acceptance

The ledger must make the counterfactual unmistakable, retain current signals and P&L, and calculate the opposite curve from the same baseline and replay window.
