Input: illustrative UK secured business lending stages and cited control considerations. No customer data, login, remote services or runtime dependencies. Actors: interview candidate / presenter.

2 · Explore the lending map

Numbered spatial nodes connect origination, validation, valuation, credit, legal security, drawdown and monitoring. Selecting a node changes the detail panel. BA highlighting or an actor role filter changes emphasis, never removes stages.

3 · Inspect a stage and its controls

Show actors, incoming evidence, outgoing deliverables, BA/product contribution, controls and interview prompts. AI recommendations require accountable human review; unresolved exceptions and unmet conditions prevent drawdown in the explained process.

4 · Play, pause, step and reset

Animated information follows ordered edges. Revaluation returns to valuation, then credit review. Pause freezes information movement. Reset restores the start, default role and emphasis. Reduced-motion preference starts paused.

5 · Discuss exceptions and outcomes

Exception management records remediation and escalation; rework returns to validation. Approval may decline or return a case; drawdown needs cleared conditions. This is explanatory visualization, not a lending execution engine.
