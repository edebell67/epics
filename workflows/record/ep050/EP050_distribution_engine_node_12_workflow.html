EP050 Node 12 — Demand Opportunity Scoring (DOS) Workflow

Stage 3 Strategy node: computes deterministic Demand Opportunity Score (DOS v1.0) and priority tiering from Node 11 classification output.

NODE 12 EXECUTION FLOW

Opportunity Scoring Pipeline

1
Ingest Intent Classification: Consume Node 11 IntentClassificationResult (primary/secondary intents, urgency, taxonomy scores).
2
Evaluate Urgency Component: Map urgency_level (high=1.0, medium=0.6, low=0.3) $\times$ 35% weight.
3
Evaluate Intent Viability Component: Score commercial / troubleshooting value $\times$ 30% weight.
4
Evaluate Service Alignment: Validate service context (e.g. boiler_repair) $\times$ 20% weight.
5
Evaluate Local Alignment: Validate locality presence $\times$ 15% weight.
6
Calculate DOS & Tier: Compute final DOS (0.0–100.0), assign priority tier (TIER_1_IMMEDIATE to TIER_4_LOW), generate deterministic opp_ ID, and preserve full lineage.
FORMULA & INVARIANTS

DOS v1.0 Formula

DOS = (Urgency*35) + (Intent*30) + (Service*20) + (Locality*15)

• Tier 1 Immediate: DOS ≥ 80.0

• Tier 2 High: 65.0 ≤ DOS < 80.0

• Tier 3 Moderate: 45.0 ≤ DOS < 65.0

• Tier 4 Low: DOS < 45.0
