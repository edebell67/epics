EP050 — Y-shaped demand discovery intake workflow

Two valid starting conditions feed one demand-to-outcome engine: an existing offer can be tested against real demand, or a market can be explored before any product exists. Both branches must produce the same validated opportunity-and-offer contract before entering the shared EP050 execution pipeline.

Evidence boundary. Branch B may generate hypotheses, never facts. A discovered app or service concept cannot enter the shared engine until real problem evidence, a defined audience, a testable proposition and explicit success/rejection criteria exist. Neither branch authorizes scraping, publishing, outreach, spend, lead routing or any other external action.
