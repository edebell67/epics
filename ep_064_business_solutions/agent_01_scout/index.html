    Rules Scout always follows

    • You stay in charge, with almost no effort. Scout sends one plan a day. On the default setting it goes ahead after an hour unless you stop it, so a normal week needs no taps. Every new business starts on "ask me first" until they trust it.
    • You can see everything at any time. The overview shows what Scout did, what it found and what's waiting.
    • Messages go from your own number and email, written in your voice. You can edit any message before it goes.
    • Cold outreach only to companies, using business addresses, always with an opt-out. Sole traders and members of the public are only contacted if they're already your customers.
    • Anyone who opts out is never contacted again, by any agent.
    • Every recent customer is asked for a review, not only the happy ones, and nobody is offered anything for one. That's Google's rule. Unhappy customers also get a way to tell you directly.
    • Referral thank-yous are fine and are kept separate from reviews.
    • It never invents a review, a result or a testimonial, and never posts anything about a customer without permission.
    The live version connects to the jobs, invoices or bookings you already keep (Xero, QuickBooks, your booking app, or a spreadsheet), your Google Business Profile and WhatsApp Business.
