Late-payment interest (business clients)

Under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998, a business that pays late owes interest at 8% over the Bank of England reference rate, plus fixed compensation of £40, £70 or £100 depending on the debt. ChaseUp never adds this for private customers.
The reference rate is the base rate on 30 June or 31 December, fixed for the next six months. For invoices due July–December 2026 that is 3.75% (11.75% in total). The base rate rose to 4% on 5 November 2026, so expect 4% from January 2027.
