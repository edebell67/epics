    Six agents, one job each

    Each agent hands over to the next, so nothing that leads to money gets dropped. Start with the one that hurts most.

    Rules Closer always follows

    • Quotes go out within hours of the visit, built from your notes or voice memo and your price list, in your template.
    • Prices only come from your price list. Anything custom comes to you once, with the maths shown, before it's sent.
    • It offers good, better and best options where it makes sense, so the customer is choosing between your options, not between you and another firm.
    • It follows up on days 3, 7 and 14, politely, then closes the quote off. Nobody gets chased more than that.
    • It never cuts your price on its own. When asked for a discount, it only offers alternatives you've pre-approved (smaller scope, own materials, payment plans).
    • Every lost quote gets a reason logged, so you can see whether you lose on price, timing or trust.
    • A "yes" becomes a booked job: date in the diary, deposit link sent, invoice handed to Collector.
    The live version uses your existing quote template, or Xero, QuickBooks or Tradify quotes, and sends from your own email address.
