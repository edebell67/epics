Rules Frontdesk always follows

  • Every enquiry gets a reply within 2 minutes, day or night: missed calls, web forms, WhatsApp, Facebook and email. The live version can also answer calls by voice.
  • It only books into free slots in your diary, and only for jobs, areas and hours you've set.
  • It only quotes prices from your price list, always as a range when the job needs seeing first. Anything else comes to you.
  • You're told straight away about emergencies and complaints. It apologises, offers a fix and books it, then lets you know.
  • It politely turns away work you don't do or areas you don't cover.
  • It always says it's your booking assistant, never pretends to be you, and hands over to you whenever a customer asks for a person.
  • It doesn't take card details. Deposits go through your own payment link.
The live version connects to your phone line or WhatsApp Business, your website form, Facebook page and your calendar or booking app.
