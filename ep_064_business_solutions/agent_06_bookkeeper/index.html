    Six agents, one job each

    Each agent hands over to the next, so nothing that leads to money gets dropped. Start with the one that hurts most.

    Rules Bookkeeper always follows

    • Every receipt is read, kept digitally and coded to a category and, where it can tell, to the job it was bought for. You never need to keep the paper.
    • When it's unsure, it uses your diary and bank feed to work it out, and asks you only if it still can't tell.
    • Personal spending is kept out of the business books.
    • Mileage is logged from the jobs in your diary at HMRC's approved rate.
    • Nothing is ever sent to HMRC without your OK, whatever setting you choose, and you can always send it to your accountant first.
    • Tax set-aside figures are estimates. Your accountant confirms the real numbers. Bookkeeper works with them, not instead of them.
    Making Tax Digital for Income Tax applies to sole traders and landlords above HMRC's income threshold. For limited companies, Bookkeeper keeps the same records ready for VAT returns and year-end accounts. The live version connects to your bank feed and a receipts inbox, and works alongside Xero, QuickBooks or FreeAgent if you use them.
