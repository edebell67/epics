What Collector did

    Six agents, one job each

    Each agent hands over to the next, so nothing that leads to money gets dropped. Start with the one that hurts most.

    Rules Collector always follows

    • Invoices go out the moment a job is accepted or finished, with a pay-by-card link, from your own email address.
    • Payments are matched from your bank feed, so nobody gets chased for money that has already landed.
    • Chasing follows your ladder: friendly on the due date, firm at 7 days, final at 14, then a warning that a formal letter will follow.
    • Late-payment interest and compensation are only added for business clients, under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts Act. Never for private customers.
    • A promise to pay pauses chasing until that date. Payment plans up to 3 months are agreed automatically if you allow them.
    • Judgement calls come to you: waiving interest, writing off a debt, or sending a real letter before claim (which, for an individual, must follow the Pre-Action Protocol).
    The live version connects to Xero, QuickBooks or your invoicing app, plus your bank feed and card payment link.
