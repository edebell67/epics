What Keeper did

    Six agents, one job each

    Each agent hands over to the next, so nothing that leads to money gets dropped. Start with the one that hurts most.

    Rules Keeper always follows

    • It knows when each customer is next due, from your history: annual services, MOTs, colours, renewals, certificates.
    • Reminders are personal and come from your number, with a link to pick a free slot, so customers book themselves.
    • One reminder and one gentle nudge, then it waits until next month. Nobody gets hassled.
    • Quiet days get offered early to customers who are due soon. Discounts only if you say so.
    • Lost quotes get a check-back after the time you set, then go back to Frontdesk if there's interest.
    • Anyone who opts out is never contacted again, by any agent.
    The live version connects to your customer list or job history, your diary or booking app, and WhatsApp Business.
