Applications

Our platforms are designed
around processes,
not industries.

An application is a process a platform has been configured to run, not a sector we claim specialist knowledge of. The same Lead Generation platform that runs local business lead generation below could be configured for a different process tomorrow without becoming a different product.

How an application relates to a platform

A platform is the reusable engine. An application is the specific process the platform is configured to run. A deployment is that configuration applied to one client, market or context. Industry examples can appear underneath a deployment, they never define the platform or the application itself.

Lead Generation Platform → Local Lead Generation → possible deployments: plumbing, electrical, roofing, cleaning, professional services, and so on — the platform is reusable, the deployment is configurable.
