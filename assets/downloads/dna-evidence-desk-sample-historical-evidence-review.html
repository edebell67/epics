Historical Evidence Review

A completed example of the monthly Evidence Desk format, using a verified non-customer historical/replay comparison. It is an illustration of research reporting—not a recommendation.

Sample period23 September 2026
Prepared26 September 2026
Evidence modeHistorical / replay
Hard boundaryThis sample examines a closed historical/replay record. It is not investment advice, a trade signal, a forecast, an income claim, or an instruction to buy, sell, or hold. No broker account, custody, order submission, or live trading was involved.

1. Research question

Within 30 isolated paired historical/replay portfolio tests on 23 September 2026, how did Buy/Sell Split (All) compare with Total Net on the recorded closed-result outcome and closed rotations?

2. Scope and controls

  • 30 matched, browser-local historical/replay tests.
  • Each test used a random 2–4 strategy portfolio drawn from the available 30-model candidate pool.
  • The same basket was tested in both modes.
  • Two batches: 10 tests, then 20 distinct additional tests.

3. What was excluded

  • No live market feed, broker connection, order submission, or execution.
  • No claim about future probability or performance.
  • Partial 22 September collection excluded: it was stopped and is invalid for interpretation.
  • No conclusion beyond this same-day sample.

5. Finding, limited to the record reviewed

Bounded finding
In these 30 matched historical/replay tests, Buy/Sell Split (All) produced the higher recorded closed result more often than Total Net (21 versus 8, with 1 tie) and recorded fewer closed rotations (213 versus 425).

Support: The result appeared in both batches: 7 of 10 Split-higher in the first batch and 14 of 20 Split-higher in the additional batch.

Counter-evidence and limit: Eight tests favoured Total Net and one tied. The sample is limited to one historical day, a defined candidate pool, a local replay environment, and the tested mode settings.

7. Replay and mode disclosure

Historical / replay

The underlying test record came from isolated in-application historical/replay portfolios. The comparison is useful as auditable evidence of what occurred in that constrained record. It cannot show live execution quality, future market behaviour, availability of an instrument, real-world fills, slippage, or suitability for any person.

8. Limitations and counter-evidence

  • Same-day evidence: this report does not claim the relationship persists across other days or conditions.
  • Sampling: portfolios were random selections from the then-available 30-model candidate pool; they are not a representative universe of all possible strategies.
  • Metric boundaries: aggregate closed result and rotation count are descriptive measures in the test record, not a complete risk assessment.
  • Invalid evidence excluded: the partial 22 September collection was not used because it did not complete.
  • No causal conclusion: the comparison does not establish why one mode was higher in the sample.

9. Conclusion

Answer to the sample question
For the defined 23 September 2026 historical/replay sample, Buy/Sell Split (All) had the higher recorded closed result in most matched tests and used fewer recorded closed rotations. That is the extent of the conclusion. It is not a forecast, a recommendation, or an execution instruction.
