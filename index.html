What we do

One engine.
One directory.
Every application.

Our objective is simple: build the applications and platforms the trading space runs on. Everything starts in our trade engine and flows through a single strategy directory, so each new app inherits the same data, the same track record and the same results.

01 · Trade engine

Strategies are traded

Our trade engine runs thousands of strategies across forex and crypto markets and records every trade they close, with real costs taken out.

02 · Key product

The strategy directory

Those results become the directory: thousands of strategies ranked on closed-trade profitability, net of real costs. It is the product at the centre of everything we build.

03 · Applications

Many apps, shared data

Each app performs a different function, from research and AI search to competition and agent simulation, yet all share the same trades and strategy directory.

04 · Delivery

Businesses, traders, agents

Licensed and white-labelled to trading businesses, used directly by retail traders, and consumed programmatically by AI agents.

How we work

Problem to platform.
No theatre.

Not a workflow we describe in a pitch deck, one we actually run, on every build, that leaves a record behind. Plan, design, implementation: each stage has its own discipline, and each one is enforced by a skill, not a good intention.

See the build record→
01

Plan

Every task becomes a timestamped, documented record with a required evidence section built in from the start, not filled in after the fact, and moves through a real lifecycle: backlog, in progress, complete.

02

Design

Non-trivial work gets a real process map before it gets built: a top-down hierarchy of nodes, each with its own completion test, so the build has a spec to be checked against, not just a memory of what was intended.

03

Build

Multiple models can work the same codebase without corrupting each other's changes, coordinated through a shared, append-only message board instead of luck.

04

Prove

Every claim of "done" needs typed evidence attached, a test result, a live URL, a log, graded against what it actually proves. A task isn't complete because someone said so; it's complete because the evidence says so.
