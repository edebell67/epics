and Node 18 (Video Asset Factory) that removes the human-manual-assembly step their original,
already-accepted `generate_and_register()` methods required. Node 11-14 and Node 17 (Gemini-owned)

assembled/passed the resulting objects. Node 16 facts are queried from the real store, never
fabricated -- canonical business-knowledge curation remains a human/process responsibility outside
this task's scope, same as Node 01-04's business facts were correctly left untouched in the prior
Nodes 05-10 task.
