Business Automation.

A reusable engine that automates customer interaction, appointment booking, qualification, CRM and pipeline workflows, notifications and reporting, operational load taken off a team, end to end, with a visible audit trail rather than a black box.

The problem

A customer enquiry that arrives at 11pm, or a lead that goes quiet after the first reply, is usually lost, not because the business didn't want it, but because nothing was watching for it outside business hours or after the first non-response. Booking, qualification and follow-up end up split across a contact form, a shared inbox and someone's memory, with no record of what was recovered and what wasn't.

What it does

Operational load off a team,
with a visible trail.

The platform answers and qualifies customer interactions automatically, books or routes what needs a human, and follows up on anything that goes cold, every step logged so the business can see exactly what was recovered and what wasn't.

01

Interact

A trained assistant answers real questions in the business's own voice, at any hour, grounded in its actual services, prices and availability.

02

Qualify & book

Enquiries are qualified automatically and routed into booking, callback or CRM pipeline workflows without manual triage.

03

Follow up & report

Non-converting leads are followed up automatically, and every recovered enquiry is reported with a figure attached.

Inputs

  • The business's actual services, pricing, hours and availability
  • Incoming customer interactions — site chat, forms, booking requests
  • Existing CRM/pipeline state, where a deployment integrates with one
  • Configured qualification and follow-up rules per deployment

Workflow

Customer Interaction → AI Assistant Response → Qualification → Appointment Booking / Callback Routing → CRM / Pipeline Update → Notifications → Cold-Lead Follow-Up → Reporting.

Automation

  • The assistant is grounded and deployed against a business's own content without manual scripting per client
  • Qualification and routing rules are applied automatically to every interaction
  • Follow-up on non-converting leads is triggered on a schedule, not remembered by a person
  • Recovered-revenue reporting is computed from actual follow-up outcomes, not estimated

Outputs

  • Answered and qualified customer interactions, logged with full context
  • Booked appointments and routed callbacks
  • Updated CRM/pipeline records
  • A recovered-revenue dashboard showing what follow-up actually brought back

Intelligence / data layer

Every interaction, qualification decision and follow-up outcome is logged in a per-client admin dashboard, the platform is built as a modular, multi-tenant system so capabilities can be switched on per client without a separate build each time.

Integrations

  • Website Automation platform — the assistant is embedded directly on the deployed site
  • Lead Generation platform — qualified interactions hand off as leads with attribution intact
  • Existing CRM/pipeline tools, where a deployment integrates rather than replaces

Measurement

  • Interactions handled and qualified per period
  • Appointments booked and callbacks routed
  • Cold-lead recovery rate and recovered revenue
  • Response time outside business hours versus before deployment

Feedback loop

Questions the assistant can't answer and interactions that don't convert are surfaced back to the business and, where connected, back into Demand Intelligence and Content & Distribution, an unanswered question is itself a demand signal.
