Content & Distribution.

A reusable engine that turns demand-led topic discovery into generated content and video, routes it through the right channels, and closes the loop with attribution and feedback, content that is routed by evidence of demand, not a publishing calendar.

The problem

Most content programmes run on a calendar, not on evidence, topics get chosen because a slot needs filling, published to whatever channel is habitual, and then left with no link back to whether they answered a real question or reached anyone who had it. Video, written content and distribution end up as separate workstreams with separate owners, so nothing routes traffic between them or tells you which asset actually earned its production cost.

What it does

Content routed by demand,
not a calendar.

The platform starts from a real demand signal, generates the content and video asset that answers it, distributes across the channels that signal came from, and measures whether it actually closed the gap, then feeds that measurement back into the next topic decision.

01

Discover topic

A demand-led topic or question arrives from Demand Intelligence, with a source and a confidence measure attached.

02

Generate & route

Content and video assets are generated against the topic and routed to the channels the demand signal actually came from.

03

Attribute & feed back

Traffic and outcomes are attributed to the originating asset and channel, and the result reprioritises the next topic.

Inputs

  • Demand-led topics, questions and opportunities from the Demand Intelligence platform
  • Existing content and video inventory, to avoid duplicating coverage
  • Verified facts and source material the generated content must ground itself in
  • Configured channel list and publishing destinations per deployment

Workflow

Demand-Led Topic Discovery → Content Generation → Video Automation → Short-Form Content → Multi-Channel Publishing → Distribution Intelligence → Destination Mapping → Traffic Attribution → Feedback.

Automation

  • Topic selection is driven directly by the Demand Intelligence backlog, not a manual editorial calendar
  • Content and video generation run from verified source facts, with a plausibility gate before publish
  • Publishing to each configured channel is scheduled and executed automatically
  • Attribution is computed per asset and channel without manual tagging

Outputs

  • Published content and video assets, each traceable to its source demand signal
  • Destination and landing pages the content routes to
  • Per-asset, per-channel traffic and attribution data
  • A reprioritised topic backlog for the next generation cycle

Intelligence / data layer

Every asset this platform produces is generated against a specific, evidenced demand signal from the shared intelligence layer, not a topic chosen by instinct, so distribution performance can be traced all the way back to the demand that justified making the asset in the first place.

Integrations

  • Demand Intelligence platform — source of every topic and question
  • Website Automation platform — destination and landing pages for distributed content
  • Lead Generation platform — shares distribution and attribution infrastructure

Measurement

  • Content and video assets generated and published per period
  • Traffic and engagement attributed per asset and channel
  • Demand-signal-to-published-asset cycle time
  • Share of published assets that trace to a measurable downstream outcome

Feedback loop

Attribution data determines which topics, formats and channels actually closed the demand gap they were built for, and that result reprioritises the next round of topic discovery, the same discipline the Distribution Engine runs at platform scale.
