Platform · 01

Lead Generation.

A reusable engine that automates the path from discovering demand to creating measurable leads, demand discovery, content, distribution, capture, qualification and attribution as one connected system, configurable for any process, market or client.

The problem

Most lead generation is a sequence of disconnected activities, a content calendar here, an ad campaign there, a contact form nobody follows up on. Nothing tells you which activity actually produced the lead, so budget keeps going to whatever felt busiest rather than whatever worked. Demand signals that exist in search behaviour, market conversations and customer questions rarely make it back into what gets built or published next.

What it does

One connected system,
not a stack of tools.

The platform discovers demand, turns it into content and destinations, distributes that content, captures and qualifies the resulting leads, and attributes every lead back to the signal that produced it, then feeds that attribution back into what gets discovered and built next.

01

Discover & create

Demand and pain-point discovery feed content-opportunity discovery and content creation, every asset is built against a real, evidenced signal.

02

Distribute & capture

Content is routed to destination and landing pages; visitors are captured, qualified and scored automatically as leads.

03

Attribute & improve

Every lead is attributed back to its source signal; analytics and feedback reprioritise the next round of demand discovery.

Inputs

  • Search and question-demand signals from the Demand Intelligence platform
  • Existing content, product and pricing information for the deploying business
  • Configured product_forex-style whitelist of products, models or service lines in scope
  • Prior lead and attribution history, where it exists

Workflow

Demand Discovery → Pain-Point Identification → Content Opportunity Discovery → Content Creation → Distribution → Landing / Destination Pages → Lead Capture → Qualification → Attribution → Analytics → Feedback / Optimisation.

Automation

  • Demand and opportunity discovery run on a schedule, not on request
  • Content generation and routing are triggered directly by discovered opportunities
  • Lead scoring, qualification and attribution are computed automatically on capture
  • Under-performing content and channels are flagged for the feedback loop without manual review

Outputs

  • Published content and destination/landing pages
  • Captured, qualified leads with a source attribution record
  • Attribution and performance analytics per channel and content asset
  • A prioritised backlog of the next demand-led content opportunities

Intelligence / data layer

Lead Generation consumes structured demand signals from the Demand Intelligence platform rather than discovering demand independently, the two platforms share one intelligence layer so a signal discovered once can feed content, product and distribution decisions across every deployment.

Integrations

  • Website Automation platform — for destination and landing pages
  • Content & Distribution platform — for content creation and multi-channel publishing
  • Business Automation platform — for qualification handoff and CRM/pipeline workflows

Measurement

  • Leads captured and qualified per period
  • Attribution coverage — the share of leads traced to a specific demand signal
  • Content-to-lead conversion rate by asset and channel
  • Cost or effort per qualified lead, where deployment data supports it

Feedback loop

Attribution and conversion data are routed back into demand-discovery prioritisation, so the platform's own output becomes an input to what it discovers and builds next, the same PROBLEM → INTELLIGENCE → ENGINEERING → PLATFORM → AUTOMATION → OUTCOME → MEASUREMENT → FEEDBACK loop that runs across every platform.
