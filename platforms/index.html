Platforms

Six reusable engines.
Any process, any market.

A platform combines capabilities, AI, automation, data, APIs, workflow orchestration, into a reusable system. The platform is then configured for a particular application and deployed for a client, market or industry. That deployment is the configuration, not the expertise: none of the six platforms below claim specialist knowledge of the industries they end up running in.

01

Lead Generation

Automates the path from discovering demand to creating measurable opportunities. Demand discovery, content, distribution and capture as one connected system.

  • Demand and pain-point discovery
  • Content creation and distribution
  • Landing pages, lead capture and qualification
  • Attribution, analytics and feedback

What we build toward A repeatable engine that finds demand and converts it, not a single campaign.
