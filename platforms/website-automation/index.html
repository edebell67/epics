Website Automation.

A reusable engine for generating and rebuilding websites, adding AI search visibility and AI assistants, and wiring in booking and conversion systems. The site is one node in a larger automation system, not the whole engagement, and not a claim of design-agency specialism on its own.

The problem

A website is usually delivered as a finished object and then left alone, template-assembled, disconnected from search visibility, unable to answer a visitor's actual question at 11pm, and with no route back into whatever demand or content system is supposed to be feeding it. Legacy rebuilds compound the problem: years of accumulated content and search equity get put at risk by a redesign that treats migration as an afterthought.

What it does

A site that's a node,
not the whole engagement.

The platform generates or rebuilds the site itself, then wires it into search visibility, an AI assistant, and the booking/conversion systems that turn a visit into an outcome, with the whole thing hand-built rather than assembled from a stack of plugins.

01

Generate or rebuild

A hand-built design system is generated for the business, or an existing legacy site is rebuilt with its content and search equity preserved.

02

Make it visible

Technical SEO, structured data and AI search visibility are built in from the start, not bolted on afterward.

03

Convert & measure

An AI assistant, booking flow and analytics turn traffic into a measured outcome, with a dashboard showing whether it worked.

Inputs

  • Business content, services, pricing and hours for the deploying business
  • Existing site content and URL structure, where a rebuild is preserving search equity
  • Positioning and taxonomy rules, so the generated copy never overclaims industry expertise
  • Demand and topic data from the Demand Intelligence platform, where connected

Workflow

Content & Audit Input → Design-System Generation or Legacy Migration → Template & Component Build → Technical SEO & Structured Data → AI Assistant Wiring → Booking / Conversion Systems → Analytics → QA & Launch.

Automation

  • Design-system components and page templates are generated from a shared content schema, not hand-copied per site
  • Structured data, sitemap and metadata are generated automatically from page content
  • The AI assistant is grounded and deployed against the site's own content without manual scripting per client
  • Redirects for a legacy rebuild are generated from the old-to-new URL mapping, not typed by hand

Outputs

  • A hand-built, fast, accessible website owned outright by the business
  • A trained AI assistant live on the site, capturing and following up on leads
  • Technical SEO baseline: sitemap, structured data, canonical tags, Open Graph metadata
  • Analytics and conversion-event tracking wired to real actions on the site

Intelligence / data layer

Where a deployment is connected to Demand Intelligence, page and content priorities are informed by real search-demand data rather than guesswork; where it isn't, the platform still ships with its own analytics layer so decisions have evidence behind them from day one.

Integrations

  • Demand Intelligence platform — for search-intent and topic-priority input
  • Content & Distribution platform — for ongoing content generation and publishing
  • Business Automation platform — for booking, callback and CRM handoff
  • First-party analytics and AI-assistant embed, deployed per site

Measurement

  • Core Web Vitals and load performance
  • Organic impressions, clicks and indexed pages
  • Assistant-handled enquiries and recovered-revenue from follow-up on non-converters
  • Conversion events per CTA and form

Feedback loop

Analytics and assistant conversation data are routed back into content and structural decisions, underperforming pages and unanswered assistant questions become the next round of content or product work, not a report that sits unread.
