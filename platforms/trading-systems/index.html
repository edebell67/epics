Trading Systems.

A reusable engine for organising trading intelligence, comparing strategies, and automating execution and monitoring workflows. This is not a claim of expertise in any particular market, it is an engineering capability for building the infrastructure a trading operation runs on.

The problem

A strategy that looks good in a backtest routinely fails to hold up live, not because the idea was wrong, but because nothing in the pipeline was watching for the gap between the two. Strategy creation, comparison, execution and monitoring are usually separate tools glued together by hand, so a single scaling defect or a stale threshold can sit undetected until it has already cost real money.

What it does

Discovery, comparison and
execution, one pipeline.

The platform generates and organises candidate strategies, compares them against each other on evidence rather than assumption, and automates the execution and monitoring workflows so a strategy's live behaviour is watched as closely as its backtest.

01

Discover

Candidate strategies and models are generated and catalogued from live and historical market data.

02

Compare

Strategies are ranked on closed-trade evidence, net return, win rate, profit factor, recalculated from the underlying trade history, not a fixed snapshot.

03

Execute & monitor

Selected strategies are executed automatically, with drawdown, performance and scaling behaviour monitored continuously.

Inputs

  • Live and historical market/price data for the configured instruments
  • Candidate strategy and model definitions
  • Closed and open trade history
  • Configured risk limits — per-model position caps, max open trades, drawdown thresholds

Workflow

Market Data Ingestion → Strategy Discovery → Strategy Directory / Intelligence → Signal Generation → Backtesting → Strategy Selection → Execution Automation → Risk / Drawdown Monitoring → Performance Analytics → Strategy Monitoring.

Automation

  • Candidate trade generation runs on a continuous loop against live market data
  • Strategy comparison recalculates from the underlying closed-trade evidence on every query, not a cached report
  • Execution and position-limit gating are enforced automatically, not by manual sign-off
  • Drawdown and scaling-defect monitoring flags anomalies without waiting for a scheduled review

Outputs

  • A ranked strategy directory with live-recalculated evidence
  • Executed and monitored open positions with an audit trail
  • Performance and drawdown analytics per strategy and per model
  • Anomaly and scaling-defect alerts

Intelligence / data layer

Every strategy comparison is computed directly from closed-trade history rather than a periodic snapshot, the Strategy Directory below queries the live database on every request, so the ranking always reflects the current evidence, not last week's export.

Integrations

  • Market data and price-collection services feeding live signal generation
  • SQL Server trade database as the system of record for open and closed trades
  • Analytics dashboards over the same data for performance and drawdown review

Measurement

  • Net return, win rate and profit factor per strategy, recalculated live
  • Open and closed trade counts per model and per strategy family
  • Drawdown against configured risk limits
  • Query and execution latency of the underlying data pipeline

Feedback loop

Live execution and monitoring data feed straight back into strategy comparison and selection, so underperforming or misbehaving strategies are demoted by evidence rather than by manual review, the same discipline that surfaced and fixed a real scaling defect in this platform's own trade-creation pipeline during development.
